Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-04
  Features:
    - BUS variants of every pipe and underground pipe in the game, including pipes from other mods.
      Same properties as the original, marked with a spray-painted black "B".
    - Bus coupler: joins all segments of BUS pipes into one network and keeps their fill level even,
      so the whole bus behaves like one tank. Connects only to BUS pipes and needs electricity.
    - Fluid amount and temperature are preserved exactly while balancing.
    - The coupler outputs the bus fill (%) and the total fluid amount to the circuit network.
    - Coupler status in its window: network size and fill, no power, not connected, mixed fluids.
    - Startup settings: coupler power and balancing interval.
  Compatibility:
    - Tested with Space Age, Pyanodon's modpack, Bob's Logistics and Pipe Tiers.
